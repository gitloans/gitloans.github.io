Crypto knowledge centre

Learn about USDT, lending & collateral

Educational summaries for people researching crypto loans. This information is general and is not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

What is a cryptocurrency loan?

A crypto loan is an arrangement in which a lender provides money or digital assets under agreed repayment terms. Some arrangements require crypto collateral. Check the lender, fees, repayment conditions, and default process.

What is USDT (Tether)?

USDT is a stablecoin designed to track the US dollar and is available on multiple blockchain networks. Confirm the supported network and address before any transfer. Issuer, platform, depegging, and regulatory risks remain.

How does interest work?

Interest may be calculated as a flat percentage of the original principal or by compounding. Ask for the monthly amount, total cost over the expected term, and consequences of missed payments in writing.

What is loan-to-value (LTV)?

LTV compares the loan amount with the value of collateral. A higher LTV can leave less room for a market decline before collateral rules are triggered. Confirm valuation methods and liquidation thresholds.

What is a smart contract?

A smart contract is code deployed on a blockchain that executes defined actions. It does not automatically guarantee a fair, funded, secure, or legally enforceable deal. Verify addresses and independent technical evidence.

What is crypto collateral?

Collateral is an asset pledged to secure a loan. The agreement should explain custody, valuation, liquidation triggers, return conditions, and what happens if prices move or a payment is missed.

How do I compare loan offers?

Compare total cost, repayment dates, collateral requirements, default terms, counterparty identity, contract details, dispute process, and whether complete written documentation is available.

How do I protect my wallet?

A public address can receive assets; a private key or recovery phrase controls access. Never share your seed phrase or private key with an administrator, support agent, website, or loan tool.
